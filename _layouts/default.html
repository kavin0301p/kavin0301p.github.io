Kavin Prakash

I am a pre-final year student of the Dual Degree (Bachelor of Science in Chemistry + Master of Technology in AI Applications) at the Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur, with a micro specialisation in High Performance Computing.

I am fortunate to conduct research at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay, as part of Intel RealSense. I primarily designed computer vision algorithms for efficient hardware ASICs, including the Intel RealSense R200 and D400 RGB-D sensors. Additionally, I worked on software APIs, active illumination systems, human-computer interaction devices, and helped develop demos for trade shows, including CES 2012-2016.

I have an MS in Computer Science (AI focus) from Stanford University, where I was a research assistant for Silvio Savarese and a teaching assistant for Fei-Fei Li (CS131 & CS231N). I have a BS in EECS from UC Berkeley, where I worked in Kris Pister's lab.
