Kavin Prakash

I am a pre-final year student of the Dual Degree (Bachelor of Science in Chemistry + Master of Technology in AI Applications) at the Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur, with a micro specialisation in High Performance Computing.

I am fortunate to conduct research across AI, speech and language technologies, with research experiences at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay, UNIL HEC Lausanne, and Nanyang Technological University.

My current research interests lie at the intersection of Speech AI, and NLP. I enjoy exploring how machine learning can be applied to challenging problems across languages and modalities. I continue to explore new frontiers in AI, with a particular interest in Music AI, and the possibilities they offer for creating intelligent and meaningful systems.

  • Developed the robustness of Tamil Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) systems using speech recordings from 60 school students, designed to reflect realistic classroom speaking conditions.
